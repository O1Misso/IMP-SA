About us

To Empower South Africans Through Integrated Innovation.

Our Mission Statement

We Don't Just Build Businesses. We Build South Africa.

IMP SA is an integrated innovation ecosystem — logistics, IT, construction, tourism, housing, healthcare, and education — all connected to create one thing: sustainable jobs for South Africans.

We empower South Africans through integrated innovation, creating sustainable growth and opportunities. Our vision is to unite industries under one brand that drives progress and prosperity.

At IMP SA, we asked a simple question: What if one group could solve many of SA's biggest problems at once?

Our answer is an integrated model. Our 7 divisions work as one: Education trains you. Logistics, IT, Construction, Tourism, Property, and Healthcare hire you.

We are powered by technology South Africa can trust — GPS and AI for logistics, BIM for sustainable building, EHR & telemedicine for healthcare, and Blockchain, Digital Identity, and AI-Monitoring to kill corruption before it starts.

This is not just growth. This is sustainable growth with integrity.

Learn once. Earn for life. With our Job Guarantee for IMP graduates.
